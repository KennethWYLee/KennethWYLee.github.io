About

Computing methods for practical financial decisions.

My work examines how optimization and data-driven methods can support portfolio selection. I teach computing and analytics courses that connect programming with finance and business applications.

02

Research

Models, data, and decisions under uncertainty.

My publications examine portfolio construction from both operational and empirical perspectives, including rebalancing, forecasting, uncertainty, and transaction costs.

01

Portfolio optimization

Portfolio rebalancing, Omega-ratio models, robust optimization, transaction costs, short selling, and asset diversification.

02

Machine learning in finance

Return forecasting, sentiment analysis, text mining, and the use of momentum and reversal strategies in portfolio construction.

03

Technology management

Technology selection, strategic foresight, and structured evaluation of emerging technologies.
