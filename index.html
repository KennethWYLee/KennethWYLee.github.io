Research

Market sentiment and portfolio allocation

This work examines how momentum and market sentiment can inform portfolio allocation. It incorporates both into a parametric portfolio policy, accounting for transaction costs and rebalancing.

PLOS One (2025) ↗

Portfolio decisions under return uncertainty

This work studies how to balance portfolio gains and losses when returns are uncertain. A relative robust gain-loss model uses minimax regret within an Omega framework and is evaluated across rolling investment windows.

The Journal of Supercomputing (2025) ↗

Rebalancing under changing market conditions

This work examines intraday trading and adjustments to portfolio return thresholds. Omega portfolio models incorporate momentum and reversal strategies or dynamic return thresholds to study decisions under different market conditions.
